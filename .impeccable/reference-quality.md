# Quality references for the chosen familiar learning-app direction

The user delegated selection of reference products. These are craft references, not a mandate to copy their branding, features, accounts, or progress calculations.

- Exercism: https://exercism.org/docs/building/product/unlocking-exercises — prerequisite completion makes later exercises available. For FlashStruct, show why a stage is locked and which available action precedes it. Preserve FlashStruct's rules.
- GitBook: https://gitbook.com/docs/guides/docs-best-practices/documentation-structure-tips — navigation and an in-page contents hierarchy support technical reading. For FlashStruct, keep module and section orientation visible without competing with prose and code.
- Khan Academy: https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/115002552631-What-are-Course-and-Unit-Mastery — progress can be understood at course, unit, and skill levels. For FlashStruct, retain its own module and stage measures and clarify their labels.

## What will be evaluated

- Next action and the reason for locked stages are visible without interpreting decorative icons.
- Progress uses real local state and loaded curriculum counts.
- Desktop and mobile offer the same tasks; reading and practice use focused layouts.
- Home, curriculum, module reading, flashcards, quiz, and dashboard have compositions fitted to their different jobs.
- Theme, keyboard focus, loading, empty, error, and completed states receive the same care as the happy path.
- Account avatars, invented timers, streaks, notes, and extra courses accidentally introduced by generated previews are excluded from implementation.

The white/navy/blue visual world was chosen by the user on the direction page. Three composition options preserve it; this stage does not reopen identity.
